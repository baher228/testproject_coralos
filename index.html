FAQ

Can I use Coral Checkout on mobile?

Yes. The checkout layout, pricing cards, FAQ, and contact form are built to reflow cleanly on narrow screens.

Do I need a developer to launch?

Starter and Pro include hosted checkout pages. Scale teams can add custom onboarding if they need a deeper integration.

Can I change plans later?

You can upgrade or downgrade any time. Plan changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

Is payment information protected?

Coral Checkout is designed around secure, tokenized payment collection and clear buyer confirmation steps.
